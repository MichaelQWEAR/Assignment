It Started With a Signal

In the late 1960s, ARPANET connected the first computers through packet switching.

The idea of connection didn't stop there.

Ideas Began to Link

In 1987, HyperCard let separate pages connect to each other through hyperlinks.

One page discovering another was only the beginning.

One Network, One Web

In 1989, Tim Berners-Lee wove it all together into the World Wide Web.

And it never really stopped growing.

Now We Scroll

Today, that same idea lets a single page keep unfolding, screen by screen.
